**Method and limits.** Research ran on 6 October 2026 through a general web search tool. It is **not localised to Pakistan** and has no rank-tracking data, so treat positions as indicative. The Ahrefs and Similarweb connectors are available in this workspace but need authorising (claude.ai connector settings); with them, this analysis can be repeated with real Pakistani SERP positions and traffic estimates. Re-check in Search Console 4–8 weeks after launch.

Directories that rank for agency queries in Pakistan: **DesignRush** (413 PK digital marketing companies, updated Jul 2026), **GoodFirms** (964 PK digital marketing agencies, Aug 2026), **Clutch**, **Sortlist**, plus listicle sites. Being listed, with reviews, gets TDM into the SERP even where its own pages don't rank yet, and these pages are frequently cited by AI assistants.

## How competitors position and prove

- **Positioning:** almost all say "full-service" or "results-driven". Few have a structural differentiator. TDM's owned media network is a genuine one and is now explained on `/media-network`.
- **Service pages:** most have a single services page or thin per-service pages without pricing, process or FAQs.
- **Pricing:** established agencies hide prices ("contact us"); small shops publish cheap packages. TDM's published Rs 30,000 / Rs 70,000 packages with clear inclusions sit in a credible middle that almost nobody occupies.
- **Proof:** logo walls and big unsourced numbers. TDM's Ads Manager screenshots with text key numbers are stronger evidence than most competitors show.
- **Content:** sparse. Where blogs exist they're generic or vendor-driven, with no sources and no dates.

## SERP features

- **Local pack:** likely for "digital marketing agency lahore" style queries. Requires a verified Google Business Profile (doc 03).
- **AI Overviews / answer engines:** cost and how-to questions ("how much do facebook ads cost in pakistan", "how to pay for meta ads from pakistan") are typical AI Overview triggers. They cite pages with direct answers, tables and dated sources, which is how the six guides are written.
- **People Also Ask:** cost, payment, account-restriction and "Meta vs Google" questions. Covered by answer-first sections and FAQs.
- **Video:** YouTube results appear for how-to queries. Repurposing guides into short videos on TDM's channels is a low-cost follow-up.

## Gaps TDM can fill legitimately

1. **Transparent PKR pricing**, with clear ad-spend separation (done: `/pricing`).
2. **Neutral, sourced guides** on cost, payment, restrictions and ROAS (6 written, 20 more planned).
3. **Owned-media advertising**: the only agency offering a news-portal + social network placement product (done: `/media-network`).
4. **Real Ads Manager proof with text equivalents** (done) and full case studies once you supply results.
5. **Entity clarity**: one consistent definition across site, GBP and directories, and disambiguation from Media Times Ltd (doc 09).
6. **Directory presence with reviews** (owner action).
